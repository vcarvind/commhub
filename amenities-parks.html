Parks & Trails

Connect with nature and your neighbors in our open spaces.

Outdoor Recreation

Brookhaven is designed to be a walkable, nature-friendly community. Our network of parks and trails provides residents with endless opportunities for outdoor activities, from jogging and cycling to family picnics and bird watching.

Facility Highlights

Pavilions & BBQ

Covered pavilions equipped with BBQ grills are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Perfect for birthday parties and family reunions.

Boat Docks

Residents can launch non-motorized paddle boats and kayaks from designated community docks. Enjoy a sunset paddle on the lake.

Neighborhood Parks

In addition to the central recreational complex, smaller "pocket parks" are located throughout the villages. These typically feature playground equipment for children ages 2-12 and benches for parents.

Rules & Hours

Hours: All parks and greenbelts are open from sunrise to 10:00 PM.
Pets: Dogs must be leashed at all times. Please clean up after your pet using the provided stations.
