Social & Service

Brookhaven Ladies Club

A social and charitable organization open to all women in the community. Activities include monthly luncheons, book clubs, and bunco nights.

Contact: ladiesclub@brookhavenhub.com

Friends of the Library

Volunteers dedicated to supporting our local library through book sales and reading programs for children.
