Home Improvement

Building a better community, one home at a time.

Architectural Review

Important! Any exterior modification to your home or property must be approved by the Architectural Review Committee (ARC) before work begins.

To maintain the aesthetic standards and property values of Brookhaven, all exterior changes including painting, roofing, landscaping, fencing, and pool installations require prior approval.

Application Process

  1. Download the Application: Obtain the current Home Improvement Application form below.
  2. Gather Documents: Include a property survey, samples of materials (paint chips, roofing shingles), and detailed plans/drawings.
  3. Submit: Email your complete package to the Reviews Department or drop it off at the Association Office.
  4. Review: The committee typically reviews applications twice a month. You will be notified of the decision via mail/email.
