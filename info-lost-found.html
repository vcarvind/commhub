Lost & Found

Reconnecting items with their owners.

Have you lost or found an item?

The Brookhaven Association Office maintains a Lost & Found for items turned in by residents or found at our facilities (pools, tennis courts, parks).

How to Report

If you have found an item or are looking for one, please contact the Association Office with a detailed description.

Association Office

Phone: (555) 123-4567
Email: lostandfound@brookhavenhub.com
Address: 123 Community Blvd, Brookhaven, TX

Note on Valuables

High-value items (wallets, phones, keys) are kept in a secure location. Please be prepared to provide proof of ownership when claiming these items.
