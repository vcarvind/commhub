Community Pools

Dive into summer at our resort-style aquatic centers.

Swimming Facilities

Brookhaven offers eleven (11) pools for residents to enjoy. The pool season typically runs from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend, with select pools offering extended seasons.

Access & Guest Policy

To access the pool facilities, residents must possess a valid pool key and ID card. Each household is permitted to bring up to four (4) guests per visit. Please ensure you have your ID with you at all times when visiting the facilities.

Key Locations

  • The Beach Club: Our premier facility featuring a sandy beach entry, large lagoon-style pool, and water slides.
  • Equestrian Village Pool: A quieter, lap-focused pool perfect for exercise and relaxation.
  • Water Park: Features splash pads, buckets, and water features designed specifically for younger children.
  • Neighborhood Pools: Conveniently located within each village for easy access.
