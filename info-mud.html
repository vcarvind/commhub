MUD Districts

Providing essential utility services to our community.

What is a MUD?

A Municipal Utility District (MUD) is a political subdivision of the State of Texas authorized by the Texas Commission of Environmental Quality (TCEQ) to provide water, sewage, drainage, and other utility-related services within the MUD boundaries.

Key Responsibilities

  • Utilities: Providing clean water, sanitary sewer systems, and drainage infrastructure.
  • Trash Collection: Contracting waste management services for residential pickup.
  • Security: Often contracting annual law enforcement patrols for the district.
  • Parks: Maintaining certain recreational facilities within the district.

Know Your District

Brookhaven is served by several MUD districts depending on your specific village. It is important to know which MUD services your home.

Tax Information

MUDs are funded through property taxes and service fees. They are governed by a Board of Directors elected by the residents.
