Tennis & Pickleball

Serve up some fun with year-round court access.

Court Facilities

Brookhaven boasts 17 lighted tennis courts available for resident use year-round. Whether you are a seasoned pro or picking up a racket for the first time, our facilities cater to all skill levels.

Court Locations

  • North Lake Courts: 4 Tennis Courts. Court #3 is dual-marked for Pickleball play.
  • South Lake Courts: 2 Tennis Courts. Quiet setting near the walking trails.
  • Westside Complex: 4 Tennis Courts. Home to our annual community tournament.
  • Village Courts: Additional courts scattered throughout neighborhood pockets.

Reservations & Rules

To ensure fair access for all residents, court reservations are required during peak hours (5 PM - 10 PM on weekdays and all day weekends).

Reservation System: Residents can book courts up to 3 days in advance via the online portal.
Guest Policy: Residents must be present. Up to 3 guests allowed per court.

Pickleball Updates

Due to high demand, we have converted Court #3 at North Lake for dual use. Dedicated pickleball lines are painted in light blue. Please bring your own net adjustment straps if needed.
